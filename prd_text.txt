SECTION 1  —  CHALLENGE BRIEF & PROBLEM STATEMENT
Challenge 2: AI Service Orchestrator for Informal Economy
Pakistan's informal economy — plumbers, electricians, tutors, AC technicians, beauticians — operates through
WhatsApp, phone calls, and referrals. This causes missed opportunities, poor matching, unpredictable pricing, and no
follow-up mechanism. This challenge requires building a full agentic AI system that automates the entire service
lifecycle.
What the System MUST Do
•
Understand multilingual service requests (Urdu, Roman Urdu, English, code-switched)
•
Extract: service type, location, urgency, time preference, constraints, user preferences
•
Discover providers using mock data, Google Maps/Places, or other APIs
•
Rank providers using multi-factor matching (6+ factors) — not just distance
•
Generate dynamic price quotes with transparent breakdown
•
Simulate booking, confirmation, reminders, progress, feedback, reputation updates, dispute handling
Example Scenario (from challenge brief)
User Input
"AC bilkul kaam nahi kar raha, kal subah G-13 mein technician chahiye, budget zyada nahi hai."
Understanding
Service=AC repair | Severity=High | Location=G-13 | Time=Tomorrow morning | Budget=Sensitive
Matching
Ranks by: travel time, availability, AC specialization, on-time score, review sentiment, rate, cancellation risk
Decision
Recommends Provider A over closer Provider B — higher reliability + specialized AC repair reviews
Pricing
Visit fee + distance cost + urgency adjustment + budget-sensitive alternative shown
Simulation
Books 10AM slot → confirmation → updates booking sheet → reminder → en-route update → feedback → reputa
